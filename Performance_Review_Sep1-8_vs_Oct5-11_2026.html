Sep 1 – 8, 2026 → Oct 5 – 11, 2026 · Month‑over‑Month · 6 working days each

Revenue falls 13.7% on the same 6 working days, as pitching drops and a shrinking team converts less.

With 6 working days in each period, revenue fell from ₹38.40L (Sep 1 – 8) to ₹33.16L (Oct 5 – 11), down 13.65% — and down 13.65% per working day (₹6,39,917→₹5,52,583). Leads (+2.97%) and campaigns (+2.12%) were steady, but pitches fell 15.99% and Conversion Rate dropped from 6.19% to 5.39%, so units fell 10.37% (656→588). Head Count shrank 10.63% (160→143), cushioning Productivity (Revenue per Boe) to a 3.38% decline. Medical Cluster (+46.14%) was the only region to grow; WCC Cluster (−85.61%) and Nagpur Cluster (−26.87%) fell the most. This report covers the overall funnel, all six regions, the sub‑clusters, a new team‑level table, market‑level revenue, and day‑by‑day trends.

          Insights

          Key Takeaways

          The headline story reading all tables — including the market and daily trend data — together.
          -13.65%

          Revenue fell sharply on equal working days. Total revenue dropped from ₹38.40L to ₹33.16L (−₹5.24L); Revenue/Day fell from ₹6,39,917 to ₹5,52,583. With 6 working days in each period, this is a real decline.

          Pitches -15.99%

          Pitching, not lead supply, is the gap. Leads (+2.97%) and campaigns (+2.12%) held, but pitches fell from 5,821 to 4,890 and Pitches/Day from 970 to 815 — fewer leads reached the pitch stage.

          Conv -0.80pp

          Conversion Rate fell (6.19%→5.39%) with only Medical Cluster (+1.56pp) improving; Law + New Businesses (−2.49pp), North (−2.38pp) and WCC (−3.20pp) fell most. P to C rose 0.75pp (11.27%→12.02%) only because fewer, better‑qualified pitches were made.

          Medical +46.14%

          Medical Cluster is the bright spot. Revenue grew from ₹3.825L to ₹5.59L with no change in Head Count; Productivity rose 46.15%, P to C reached 20.25% and four of five teams grew.

          WCC -85.61%

          WCC Cluster has stalled. Revenue fell from ₹2.64L to ₹0.38L and three of its five teams had zero revenue — alone, a ₹2.26L swing equal to 43% of the company's decline.

          South -13.25%

          South Cluster, the largest region, fell ₹1.94L. FightClub (−18.41%) drove it while PackHouse (−5.24%) was resilient; Pitches fell 23.29% in the region.

          Team: 8 of 32 grew

          Declines were broad at team level. 23 of 32 teams declined; Mavericks (+₹1.22L), Apex (+₹0.70L) and PH Wolvx (+₹0.82L) were the largest gainers, T‑Pushkar (−₹0.97L), T‑Hiba (−₹0.81L) and FC Cheguevara / PH Shadow Fangs (−₹0.79L each) the largest decliners.

          Prod -3.38%

          A smaller team cushioned Productivity. Head Count fell 10.63% (160→143), so Productivity (Revenue per Boe) slipped only 3.38% (₹23,997→₹23,185) even as revenue fell 13.65%; Medical and Law + New Businesses improved it.

          New 85.52%

          The college base turned over almost entirely. 171 new colleges supplied 85.52% of Oct 5 – 11 revenue while only 28 colleges repeated — and their combined revenue fell 32.73%. The prior top‑3 colleges were absent.
