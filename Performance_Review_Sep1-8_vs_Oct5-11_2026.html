Revenue down 13.7% on equal working days, as one big region swing and a WCC collapse outweigh a Medical surge.

With 6 working days in both periods, revenue fell from ₹38.40L (Sep 1 – 8) to ₹33.16L (Oct 5 – 11), down 13.65%, and Revenue/Day fell from ₹6,39,917 to ₹5,52,583. Leads (+2.97%) and campaigns (+2.12%) edged up, but pitches fell 15.99% and units fell 10.37% (656→588). Conversion Rate fell from 6.19% to 5.39%, while Pitch‑to‑Close improved from 11.27% to 12.02% on fewer pitches. Head Count fell 10.62% (160→143), so Productivity (Revenue per Boe) slipped only 3.38%. Medical Cluster (+46.14%) was the only region to grow; WCC Cluster (−85.61%) alone accounts for 43% of the revenue decline. This report covers the overall funnel, all six regions, the Law + New Businesses and South Cluster sub‑clusters, market‑level revenue, and day‑by‑day trends.
