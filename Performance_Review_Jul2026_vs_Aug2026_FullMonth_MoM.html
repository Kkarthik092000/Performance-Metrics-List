          Insights

          Key Takeaways

          The headline story reading all tables — including the market and daily trend data — together.
          +18.34%

          Strong revenue growth on a much leaner campaign base. Total revenue rose from ₹1.41Cr to ₹1.66Cr — leads +14.5%, units +13.6% — while total campaigns fell 18.61% (9,383→7,637) and pitches held flat. Growth came almost entirely from getting more out of fewer campaigns, not from more outreach.

          -0.06pp

          Conversion Rate held essentially flat (6.70%→6.64%) even as leads grew 14.5% on far fewer campaigns — Law + New Businesses (+0.74pp) and Medical Cluster (+0.44pp) improved, while WCC (−1.03pp) and South (−0.82pp) softened, roughly offsetting each other at the overall level.

          +1.61pp

          Pitch‑to‑Close improved sharply (11.52%→13.13%) — Law + New Businesses gained 3.57pp (11.17%→14.74%) and North Cluster gained 2.34pp (11.91%→14.25%), more than offsetting WCC's decline (−1.80pp), the only region where P to C fell.

          +15.40%

          Revenue per Boe rose sharply. Head Count grew just 2.55% (157→161) while revenue grew over seven times as fast (18.34%), so Revenue per Boe rose from ₹89,605 to ₹1,03,404 — a genuine productivity gain across the full 25‑working‑day month.

          Law+NB +51.19%

          Fastest‑growing region, with the strongest efficiency gain too. Law + New Businesses revenue grew from ₹23.62L to ₹35.71L even as campaigns fell 17.84% — Conversion Rate rose 1.42pp and P to C rose 3.57pp, the strongest combination of growth and efficiency in the report.

          WCC -11.01%

          Only region to decline. WCC Cluster revenue fell from ₹17.12L to ₹15.23L even as campaigns were cut 36.30% — Conversion Rate fell from 6.91% to 6.31% and P to C fell from 15.02% to 13.22%, the only region where efficiency declined alongside revenue.

          570 → 499

          The college market narrowed while revenue grew strongly. Active colleges fell 12.46%, in sharp contrast to the 18.34% revenue growth — 203 colleges repeated from Jul 2–31 into Aug 2–31 (out of 570 active in July), with 295 new colleges contributing 44.6% of Aug's revenue.

          T-Hiba +82.7%

          Biggest team‑level mover, in both directions. T‑Hiba (WCC) grew +82.75% (₹1.57L→₹2.86L), the largest gain in the dataset, while T‑Samriddhi (also WCC) fell −83.15% (₹1.84L→₹0.31L), the steepest decline — both moves occurring within a region that declined overall.
