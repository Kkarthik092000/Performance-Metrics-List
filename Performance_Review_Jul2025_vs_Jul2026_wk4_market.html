Revenue up 18.6%, but conversion and pitch‑to‑close both fell sharply.

Revenue grew 18.61% year‑over‑year on a much bigger funnel — leads rose 57.3%, pitches rose 80.1%, while campaigns barely moved (+1.1%). That scale came at a real efficiency cost: Conversion Rate fell from 8.50% to 6.47% and Pitch‑to‑Close dropped from 16.28% to 10.82%. Headcount grew 34.8%, far faster than revenue, so Productivity per head fell 12.0%. This report breaks down the overall funnel, all six regions/departments, and a college‑level Market Analysis.

    Synthesis

    Key Takeaways

    The headline story reading the overall and regional tables together.
    +18.61%

    Revenue grew strongly, but efficiency did not keep pace. Total revenue rose from ₹93,81,200 to ₹1,11,27,500 — driven by leads up 57.3% and pitches up 80.1%, even as campaigns barely moved (+1.1%). Growth came almost entirely from working the existing campaign base harder.

    -5.46pp

    Pitch‑to‑close is the biggest casualty. P to C fell from 16.28% to 10.82% (‑33.5% relative) — WCC Cluster alone dropped over 23 points (33.41%→10.35%), the steepest efficiency collapse in the table.

    -2.03pp

    Conversion Rate also softened. Conversion fell from 8.50% to 6.47% (‑23.9% relative) — WCC (‑6.29pp) and Nagpur (‑3.05pp) drove most of the drag, while North Cluster was one of the few regions where P2C improved.

    -12.00%

    Productivity per head declined. Head Count grew 34.8% (115→155) while revenue grew only 18.6%, so Productivity (Revenue/Head) fell from ₹81,576 to ₹71,790 — the team expanded far faster than the topline it produced.

    Nagpur +104.7%

    Fastest‑growing region. Nagpur Cluster revenue more than doubled (₹9.30L→₹19.04L) on leads up 228.7% (1,528→5,023) — by far the strongest volume story, though P2C nearly halved (25.64%→12.97%) as scale increased.

    WCC -27.82%

    Steepest regional decline. WCC Cluster revenue fell from ₹19.03L to ₹13.74L despite leads growing 48.5% — a region that pulled in far more leads but converted and closed them much less effectively than a year ago.
