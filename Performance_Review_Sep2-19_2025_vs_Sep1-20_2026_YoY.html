        Insights

        Key Takeaways

        The headline story reading all tables — including the market and daily trend data — together.
        +15.10%

        Solid revenue growth on a much bigger funnel. Total revenue rose from ₹81.37L to ₹93.655L — but leads grew 32.4%, campaigns grew 21.4% and pitches grew 37.2%, all well ahead of revenue's own growth rate. The funnel scaled much faster than revenue did.

        -0.68pp

        Conversion Rate eased (6.85%→6.17%) — Nagpur Cluster's Conversion Rate collapsed 11.86pp, by far the steepest regional drop, while North Cluster (+1.05pp) and Law + New Businesses (+1.67pp) both improved.

        -1.77pp

        Pitch‑to‑Close fell (13.47%→11.70%) — Nagpur Cluster's P to C collapsed 27.27pp (38.93%→11.66%) and WCC's fell 7.64pp (17.05%→9.41%), while North Cluster gained 5.46pp, by far the largest improvement of any region.

        +2.31%

        Productivity still improved, though only modestly. Head Count grew 12.50% (120→135), close to revenue's own 15.10% growth, so Productivity (Revenue per Boe) rose only from ₹67,808 to ₹69,374 despite the softer Conversion Rate and P to C.

        North +75.88%

        Fastest‑growing region, with the strongest efficiency gain too. North Cluster revenue grew from ₹6.22L to ₹10.94L, while both Conversion Rate (+1.05pp) and P to C (+5.46pp) improved — the best combination of growth and efficiency anywhere in this report.

        WCC -73.45%

        Steepest regional decline by far. WCC Cluster revenue collapsed from ₹20.15L to ₹5.35L even as Head Count nearly halved (26→16) — Conversion Rate fell 3.77pp and P to C fell 7.64pp, the clearest underperformer in the report.

        PackHouse +122%

        Dramatic sub‑cluster reversal. Within South Cluster, PackHouse revenue more than doubled (₹6.34L→₹14.10L, ▲122.40%) while improving Conversion Rate and P to C, far outpacing FightClub's more modest +56.24% growth — PackHouse is now the dominant growth driver in South Cluster.

        Nagpur P2C -27.27pp

        Steepest efficiency collapse. Nagpur Cluster's leads exploded from 1,033 to 4,019 (+289.06%) while Conversion Rate fell from 16.84% to 4.98% and P to C fell from 38.93% to 11.66% — the clearest case of scale outrunning efficiency in this report.
