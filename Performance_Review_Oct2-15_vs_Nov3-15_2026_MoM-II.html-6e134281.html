Revenue falls 26.5% on a smaller pitch funnel, even as pitch‑to‑close rises to 10.67%.

Conversion edged down 5.35%→5.10% and pitch-to-close rose 8.94%→10.67%. Head Count shrank 147→134 (-8.84%), so Productivity declined 19.35%. Nagpur (+16.57%) was the only region to grow; Law + New Businesses lost ₹3.17L; Medical lost ₹4.29L; North lost ₹1.33L; South lost ₹4.90L, and WCC recorded zero November revenue.
