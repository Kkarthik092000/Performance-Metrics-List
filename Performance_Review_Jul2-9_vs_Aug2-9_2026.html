          Insights

          Key Takeaways

          The headline story reading all tables — including the market and daily trend data — together.
          +24.18%

          Broad revenue growth, but efficiency softened. Total revenue rose from ₹24.92L to ₹30.94L — leads +26.2%, pitches +22.1%, units +17.9% — while campaigns barely moved (+0.19%). Growth came almost entirely from working the same campaign base harder.

          -0.38pp

          Conversion Rate eased from 5.77% to 5.39% (−6.6% relative) — Nagpur Cluster fell 2.23pp (5.90%→3.67%) and WCC fell 1.12pp (6.24%→5.12%), the two biggest regional drags.

          -0.33pp

          Pitch‑to‑Close also softened (9.44%→9.11%) — WCC Cluster fell 3.37pp (10.15%→6.78%) and Nagpur fell 3.69pp (12.73%→9.04%), while Law + New Businesses (+1.66pp) and North Cluster (+2.51pp) moved the other way.

          +21.10%

          Revenue per Boe rose sharply. Head Count grew just 2.55% (157→161) while revenue grew nearly ten times as fast (24.18%), so Revenue per Boe rose from ₹15,869 to ₹19,217 — a genuine productivity gain in this short window.

          North +126.47%

          Fastest‑growing region by far. North Cluster revenue more than doubled, from ₹2.04L to ₹4.62L, even as campaigns fell 39.41% (203→123) — LPC surged from 4.38 to 10.58 and P to C rose from 8.60% to 11.11%, the strongest efficiency‑plus‑growth combination in the report.

          Nagpur -26.97%

          Only region to decline. Nagpur Cluster revenue fell from ₹5.08L to ₹3.71L even as leads grew 16.2% and campaigns grew 35.8% — Conversion Rate fell from 5.90% to 3.67% and P to C fell from 12.73% to 9.04%, the steepest efficiency drop in Table 2.

          181 → 190

          The college market barely widened. Active colleges grew only 4.97%, far behind the 24.18% revenue growth — just 40 colleges repeated from Jul 2–9 into Aug 2–9, with 150 new colleges contributing 76.3% of Aug's revenue. Over an 8‑day window, this is a market driven almost entirely by one‑off orders rather than repeat relationships.

          Titans -80.99%

          Biggest team‑level mover, in both directions. Jeevan Marg (North) grew +262.5% (₹0.32L→₹1.16L), the largest gain in the dataset, while Titans (Nagpur) fell −80.99% (₹1.42L→₹0.27L), the steepest decline. Hustlers (WCC) recorded zero activity in both periods.
