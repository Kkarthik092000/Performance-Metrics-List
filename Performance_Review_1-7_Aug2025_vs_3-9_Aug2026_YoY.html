      Insights

      Key Takeaways

      The headline story reading all tables — including the market and daily trend data — together.
      +7.69%

      Revenue grew modestly on a much bigger funnel. Total revenue rose from ₹28.73L to ₹30.94L — but leads more than doubled (+108.4%), campaigns rose 58.1% and pitches rose 78.2%. The funnel scaled far faster than revenue did.

      -4.95pp

      Conversion Rate nearly halved, from 10.34% to 5.39% (−47.9% relative) — Nagpur Cluster and WCC Cluster both saw Conversion Rate fall by more than 12 percentage points, the two steepest regional drops.

      -5.84pp

      Pitch‑to‑Close fell sharply too (14.95%→9.11%) — WCC Cluster's P to C collapsed 21.93pp (28.71%→6.78%) and Nagpur's fell 17.63pp (26.67%→9.04%), while North Cluster (+4.33pp) was the only region where P to C improved.

      -13.04%

      Productivity fell. Head Count grew 23.85% (130→161) while revenue grew only 7.69%, so Productivity (Revenue per Boe) fell from ₹22,100 to ₹19,217 — output per person is down year‑over‑year despite the much larger team and funnel.

      North +111.93%

      Best combination of growth and efficiency. North Cluster revenue more than doubled, from ₹2.18L to ₹4.62L, while both Conversion Rate (+0.35pp) and P to C (+4.33pp) improved — the only region where growth and efficiency moved together this year.

      WCC -58.26%

      Steepest regional decline. WCC Cluster revenue fell from ₹7.45L to ₹3.11L even as leads grew 50.3% and campaigns more than doubled — Conversion Rate and P to C both collapsed by double‑digit percentage points, the clearest underperformer in the report.

      141 → 190

      The market widened but retention weakened. Active colleges grew 34.75%, far outpacing the 7.69% revenue growth. Just 29 colleges repeated from Aug 2025 into Aug 2026, and their combined revenue fell 35.49% — nearly three‑quarters of Aug 2026's revenue came from colleges with no activity a year earlier.

      MWPU -₹2.67L

      Biggest single college swing. Mit World Peace University Pune, last year's top college by revenue (₹2.77L), fell to just ₹10,000 this year — a −₹2.67L drop that alone accounts for a large share of the repeat‑cohort's overall decline.
