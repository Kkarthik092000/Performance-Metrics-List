          Insights

          Key Takeaways

          The headline story reading all tables — including the market and daily trend data — together.
          +26.08%

          Broad‑based revenue growth. Total revenue rose from ₹1.12Cr to ₹1.41Cr — every input grew together: campaigns +12.3%, leads +8.2%, pitches +23.5%, units +24.4%. This wasn't one lever, it was the whole funnel moving up.

          +0.88pp

          Conversion Rate improved from 5.90% to 6.78% (+14.9% relative) even as volume grew — Medical Cluster nearly doubled its conversion rate (3.33%→5.01%) despite leads falling 18.75%, the standout efficiency story in the region table.

          +0.07pp

          Pitch‑to‑Close held essentially flat (10.61%→10.68%) despite pitches surging 23.5% — the back end of the funnel absorbed a lot more volume without losing efficiency, unlike in several recent reports where P2C fell as pitch volume rose.

          +10.82%

          Revenue per Boe rose. Head Count grew 13.8% (138→157) while revenue grew nearly twice as fast (26.1%), so Revenue per Boe rose from ₹80,855 to ₹89,605 — genuine productivity improvement, not just headcount‑driven growth.

          South +49.21%

          Fastest‑growing region. South Cluster revenue rose from ₹29.99L to ₹44.74L — already the largest region — driven almost entirely by PackHouse more than doubling (+109.42%, ₹9.55L→₹20.00L), while FightClub grew a steadier 21.1%.

          Law -9.35%

          Only clear regional soft spot. Within Law + New Businesses, the Law sub‑cluster declined 9.35% (₹11.87L→₹10.76L) with Conversion Rate falling from 8.99% to 6.87%, while New Businesses grew 19.35% — the region's modest +4.31% overall masks a real split between its two halves.

          502 → 574

          The college market widened. Active colleges grew 14.34% — 364 new colleges contributed ₹62.12L (44.2% of July's revenue), while 292 colleges from June (₹43.02L, 38.6% of June's total) didn't repeat. Repeat colleges themselves grew a healthy 14.59% (₹68.56L→₹78.56L).

          T‑Samriddhi +268%

          Biggest team‑level mover. T‑Samriddhi (WCC) grew revenue from ₹50,000 to ₹1,84,000, while two teams — PH Wolvx and T‑Hiba — launched from zero to ₹5.44L and ₹1.57L respectively. Offsetting this, Hustlers (WCC) went from ₹2.5L to a complete stand‑down.
