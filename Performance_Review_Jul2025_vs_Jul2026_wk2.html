Revenue up 13%, but conversion and pitch‑to‑close both fell hard.

    Key Takeaways

    The headline story reading the overall and regional tables together.
    +13.15%

    Revenue grew strongly, but efficiency did not keep pace. Total revenue rose from ₹59,41,700 to ₹67,23,000 — driven by campaigns more than doubling (+116.1%), leads up 39.2% and pitches up 45.4%. This was an even more scale‑driven year than prior reports.

    -2.78pp

    Pitch‑to‑close is the biggest casualty. P to C fell from 13.03% to 10.25% (‑21.3% relative) — WCC Cluster alone dropped over 18 points (29.45%→11.00%), the steepest efficiency collapse in the table.

    -1.34pp

    Conversion Rate also softened. Conversion fell from 7.52% to 6.18% (‑17.8% relative) — Nagpur (‑5.88pp) and WCC (‑5.85pp) drove most of the drag, while North Cluster was the one region where it improved (+0.53pp).

    -16.05%

    Productivity per head declined sharply. Head Count grew 34.8% (115→155) while revenue grew only 13.2%, so Productivity (Revenue/Head) fell from ₹51,667 to ₹43,374 — the team expanded far faster than the topline it produced.

    Nagpur +101.0%

    Fastest‑growing region. Nagpur Cluster revenue more than doubled (₹6.32L→₹12.70L) on leads up 304.6% (844→3,415) — by far the strongest volume story, though it came with a near‑6‑point conversion drop as scale increased.

    WCC -19.08%

    Steepest regional decline. WCC Cluster revenue fell from ₹10.98L to ₹8.89L despite leads growing 64.1% — a region that pulled in far more leads but converted and closed them much less effectively than a year ago.
