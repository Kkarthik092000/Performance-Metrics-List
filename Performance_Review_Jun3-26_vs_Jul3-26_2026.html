        Synthesis

        Key Takeaways

        The headline story reading all four tables together.
        +27.65%

        Strongest revenue growth of any report so far. Total revenue rose from ₹87,17,500 to ₹1,11,27,500 even as campaigns fell 14.8% — leads still rose 10.9% and pitches rose 22.1%, meaning the funnel got dramatically more efficient rather than just bigger.

        +30.26%

        Leads Per Campaign jumped sharply. LPC rose from 4.23 to 5.51 — fewer campaigns (6,703→5,710) produced meaningfully more leads each, the standout efficiency story in this report.

        +0.83pp

        Conversion Rate and Pitch‑to‑Close both improved. Conversion rose from 5.64% to 6.47% and P to C rose from 10.39% to 10.82% — unlike most prior reports, efficiency gains came alongside growth rather than at its expense.

        +9.53%

        Revenue Per Boe rose. Head Count grew 16.5% (133→155), but revenue grew even faster (27.65%), so Revenue Per Boe rose from ₹65,545 to ₹71,790 — output per person improved this period.

        South +50.05%

        Fastest‑growing region. South Cluster revenue rose from ₹23.19L to ₹34.79L — already the largest region by revenue — with both FightClub (+24.3%) and PackHouse (+104.3%) contributing, and Conversion Rate and P to C both improving.

        Law -7.1%

        Only clear regional soft spot. Within Law + New Businesses, the Law sub‑cluster declined 7.1% (₹9.29L→₹8.63L) while New Businesses grew 22.8% — the region's modest overall growth (+7.1%) masks a split story between its two halves.
