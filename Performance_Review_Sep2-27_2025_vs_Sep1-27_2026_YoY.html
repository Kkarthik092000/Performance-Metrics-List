        Key Takeaways

        The headline story reading all tables — including the market and daily trend data — together.
        +14.38%

        Solid revenue growth on a much bigger funnel. Total revenue rose from ₹120.83L to ₹138.21L — but leads grew 34.87%, campaigns grew 24.62% and pitches grew 39.03%, all well ahead of revenue's own growth rate. The funnel scaled much faster than revenue did.

        -0.84pp

        Conversion Rate eased (6.82%→5.98%) — Nagpur Cluster's Conversion Rate collapsed 10.75pp, by far the steepest regional drop, while Law + New Businesses (+1.60pp) and North Cluster (+0.75pp) both improved.

        -1.95pp

        Pitch‑to‑Close fell (12.95%→11.00%) — Nagpur Cluster's P to C collapsed 23.77pp (35.24%→11.47%) and WCC's fell 5.99pp (15.44%→9.45%), while North Cluster gained 3.18pp, by far the largest improvement of any region.

        -15.79%

        Productivity fell sharply. Head Count grew 35.83% (120→163), far outpacing revenue's own 14.38% growth, so Productivity (Revenue per Boe) fell from ₹1,00,692 to ₹84,791 despite overall revenue still growing.

        North +77.78%

        Fastest‑growing region, with genuine efficiency gains too. North Cluster revenue grew from ₹8.64L to ₹15.36L, while both Conversion Rate (+0.75pp) and P to C (+3.18pp) improved — the best all‑round performance anywhere in this report.

        WCC -76.34%

        Steepest regional decline by far. WCC Cluster revenue collapsed from ₹30.21981L to ₹7.15L even as Head Count less than halved (26→12) — Conversion Rate fell 3.75pp and P to C fell 5.99pp, the clearest underperformer in the report.

        PackHouse +93%

        Dramatic sub‑cluster reversal. Within South Cluster, PackHouse revenue nearly doubled (₹10.38L→₹20.07L, ▲93.35%) while improving Conversion Rate and P to C, outpacing FightClub's more modest +52.16% growth — PackHouse is now the dominant growth driver in South Cluster.

        Nagpur P2C -23.77pp

        Steepest efficiency collapse. Nagpur Cluster's leads exploded from 1,601 to 5,884 (+267.52%) while Conversion Rate fell from 15.80% to 5.05% and P to C fell from 35.24% to 11.47% — the clearest case of scale outrunning efficiency in this report.
