Revenue eases 2.6% month‑over‑month, on a leaner but noticeably more efficient funnel.

This report covers the overall funnel, all six regions, the Law + New Businesses and South Cluster sub‑clusters, and market‑level revenue (overall and by region), plus day‑by‑day trends.

        Insights

        Key Takeaways

        The headline story reading all tables — including the market and daily trend data — together.
        -2.60%

        A modest revenue pullback on a leaner, more efficient funnel. Total revenue eased from ₹141.895L to ₹138.21L — but Total Campaigns fell 15.07% while Total Leads still grew 2.68%, driving Leads Per Campaign up 20.98%. Less outreach volume produced roughly the same lead flow.

        -0.47pp

        Conversion Rate eased (6.45%→5.98%) — Medical Cluster's Conversion Rate fell 1.19pp and Nagpur Cluster's fell 1.33pp, while South Cluster (+0.65pp) was the only region to post a genuine gain.

        -0.47pp

        Pitch‑to‑Close eased (11.47%→11.00%) — WCC Cluster's P to C fell 2.32pp and Medical Cluster's fell 2.26pp, while South Cluster (+1.07pp) and Law + New Businesses' New Businesses sub‑cluster (+1.33pp) both improved.

        -3.79%

        Productivity fell in step with revenue. Head Count stayed nearly flat (161→163, +1.24%), so Productivity (Revenue per Boe) fell from ₹88,133.54 to ₹84,791.41 — almost entirely a revenue effect rather than a staffing one.

        South +32.23%

        Strongest region, with genuine efficiency gains too. South Cluster revenue grew from ₹39.40L to ₹52.10L, while both Conversion Rate (+0.65pp) and P to C (+1.07pp) improved — the best all‑round regional performance this month, driven broadly by both FightClub (+37.47%) and PackHouse (+24.66%).

        WCC -44.62%

        Steepest regional decline by far. WCC Cluster revenue fell from ₹12.91L to ₹7.15L as Head Count shrank from 20 to 12 — Conversion Rate fell 1.09pp and P to C fell 2.32pp, the clearest underperformer this month.

        Law -20.01%

        A split inside Law + New Businesses. New Businesses grew revenue 26.61% (₹15.52L→₹19.65L) while Law fell 20.01% (₹15.42L→₹12.335L) — the two sub‑clusters moved in opposite directions, netting only a modest 3.38% regional gain.

        North Prod -39.17%

        Steepest productivity decline. North Cluster's revenue fell 17.71% while Head Count grew 35.29% (17→23), driving Productivity down from ₹1,09,794 to ₹66,783 — the clearest case of headcount outrunning output this month.
