          Insights

          Key Takeaways

          The headline story reading all tables — including the market and daily trend data — together.
          +26.48%

          Broad revenue growth on a leaner campaign base. Total revenue rose from ₹48.09L to ₹60.83L — leads +19.3%, pitches +19.7%, units +20.6% — while total campaigns actually fell 4.5% (3,948→3,772). Growth came from doing more with fewer campaigns, not more of them.

          +0.06pp

          Conversion Rate held steady at 5.73%→5.79% even as the funnel scaled up sharply — a much better outcome than a typical growth‑vs‑efficiency trade‑off, driven mainly by North Cluster and Law + New Businesses both improving.

          +0.08pp

          Pitch‑to‑Close also held steady (9.48%→9.56%) — Law + New Businesses gained 2.47pp (9.02%→11.49%) and North Cluster gained 2.66pp (9.24%→11.90%), offsetting declines in Nagpur (−3.80pp) and WCC (−2.92pp).

          +23.34%

          Revenue per Boe rose sharply. Head Count grew just 2.55% (157→161) while revenue grew ten times as fast (26.48%), so Revenue per Boe rose from ₹30,631 to ₹37,780 — a genuine productivity gain across the full 11‑working‑day period.

          North +82.04%

          Best combination of growth and efficiency. North Cluster revenue nearly doubled, from ₹4.51L to ₹8.21L, even as campaigns fell 37.77% (368→229) — LPC more than doubled (4.85→10.58) and P to C rose 2.66pp (9.24%→11.90%).

          Nagpur -26.34%

          Only region to decline. Nagpur Cluster revenue fell from ₹10.25L to ₹7.55L even as leads grew 14.6% and campaigns grew 38.3% — Conversion Rate fell from 6.52% to 4.20% and P to C fell from 12.99% to 9.19%, the steepest efficiency drop in Table 2.

          288 → 286

          The college market held steady in size but churned heavily. Active colleges eased 0.69%, far behind the 26.48% revenue growth — only 81 colleges repeated from Jul 2–15 into Aug 2–16 (out of 288 active in July), with 205 new colleges contributing 65.6% of Aug's revenue.

          FC Cheguevara +259.6%

          Biggest team‑level mover, in both directions. FC Cheguevara (South) grew +259.57% (₹0.94L→₹3.38L), the largest gain in the dataset, while T‑Samriddhi (WCC) fell −76.00% (₹0.75L→₹0.18L) and Titans (Nagpur) fell close behind at −75.79% (₹2.85L→₹0.69L).
