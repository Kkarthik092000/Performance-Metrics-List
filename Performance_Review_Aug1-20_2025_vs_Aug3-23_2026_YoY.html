      Insights

      Key Takeaways

      The headline story reading all tables — including the market and daily trend data — together.
      +25.97%

      Solid revenue growth, outpaced by an even bigger funnel. Total revenue rose from ₹85.58L to ₹1.08Cr — but leads grew 47.9% and campaigns grew 57.5%, both nearly double revenue's own growth rate. The funnel scaled much faster than revenue did.

      -1.05pp

      Conversion Rate fell, from 7.50% to 6.45% (−14.0% relative) — Nagpur Cluster and WCC Cluster both saw Conversion Rate fall by more than 5.5 percentage points, the two steepest regional drops.

      -3.00pp

      Pitch‑to‑Close fell sharply (13.76%→10.76%) — WCC Cluster's P to C collapsed 16.02pp (23.77%→7.75%) and Nagpur's collapsed 16.06pp (28.05%→11.99%), while North Cluster (+5.60pp) was the only region where P to C improved meaningfully.

      +1.71%

      Productivity was essentially flat. Head Count grew 23.85% (130→161), nearly matching revenue's 25.97% growth, so Productivity (Revenue per Boe) rose only slightly, from ₹65,831 to ₹66,960 — the team grew in step with revenue rather than driving efficiency gains.

      Law+NB +127.86%

      Fastest‑growing region, with efficiency intact. Law + New Businesses revenue more than doubled, from ₹10.84L to ₹24.70L, while both Conversion Rate (+2.85pp) and P to C (+1.24pp) improved — a rare combination of scale and efficiency gain given the size of the increase.

      WCC -52.43%

      Steepest regional decline by far. WCC Cluster revenue fell from ₹20.58L to ₹9.79L even as campaigns grew 75.9% — Conversion Rate and P to C both collapsed by more than 5.5 and 16 percentage points respectively, the clearest underperformer in the report.

      305 → 398

      The college market widened faster than revenue. Active colleges grew 30.49%, slightly ahead of the 25.97% revenue growth. Only 101 colleges repeated from Aug 1–20, 2025 into Aug 3–23, 2026 (out of 305 active in 2025), with 296 new colleges contributing 55.5% of this year's revenue.

      MWPU -₹3.70L

      Biggest single college swing. Mit World Peace University Pune, last year's top college by revenue (₹4.82L), fell to ₹1.12L this year — a −₹3.70L drop that alone accounts for most of the repeat‑cohort's overall decline.
