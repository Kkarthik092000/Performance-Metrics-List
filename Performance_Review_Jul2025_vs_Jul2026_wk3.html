More volume, weaker efficiency — conversion and pitch‑to‑close both fell hard.

Revenue grew 4.4% year‑over‑year on much bigger funnel volume — leads up 33.4%, campaigns up 18.9%, pitches up 42.6% — but that scale came at a real efficiency cost: Conversion Rate fell from 7.52% to 5.92% and Pitch‑to‑Close dropped from 13.38% to 9.85%. Headcount grew 23.5%, faster than revenue, so productivity per head fell 15.5%. This report breaks down the overall funnel and all six regions/departments.

    Key Takeaways

    The headline story reading the overall and regional tables together.
    +4.38%

    Revenue grew on volume, not efficiency. Total revenue rose from ₹32,65,500 to ₹34,08,500 — but it took 33.4% more leads, 18.9% more campaigns and 42.6% more pitches to get there. This was a scale‑driven year, not an efficiency‑driven one.

    -3.53pp

    Pitch‑to‑close is the biggest casualty. P to C fell from 13.38% to 9.85% (‑26.4% relative) even as pitches rose sharply — WCC Cluster alone dropped nearly 20 points (30.65%→10.90%), the steepest efficiency collapse in the table.

    -1.60pp

    Conversion Rate also softened. Conversion fell from 7.52% to 5.92% (‑21.3% relative) — Nagpur (‑5.50pp) and WCC (‑5.89pp) drove most of the drag, while North and Law + New Businesses actually improved.

    -15.47%

    Productivity per head declined. Head Count grew 23.5% (115→142) while revenue grew only 4.4%, so Productivity (Revenue/Head) fell from ₹28,396 to ₹24,004 — the team expanded faster than the topline it produced.

    Nagpur +101.2%

    Fastest‑growing region. Nagpur Cluster revenue more than doubled (₹3.44L→₹6.92L) on leads up 310.8% (492→2,021) — by far the strongest volume story, though it came with a 5.5‑point conversion drop as scale increased.

    WCC -34.68%

    Steepest regional decline. WCC Cluster revenue fell from ₹6.43L to ₹4.20L despite leads growing 34.2% — a region that pulled in more leads but converted and closed them far less effectively than a year ago.
