        Insights

        Key Takeaways

        The headline story reading all tables — including the market and daily trend data — together.
        +7.25%

        Modest revenue growth on a much bigger funnel. Total revenue rose from ₹1.55Cr to ₹1.66Cr — but leads grew 31.2% and campaigns grew 42.1%, both several times revenue's own growth rate. The funnel scaled much faster than revenue did.

        -1.27pp

        Conversion Rate fell, from 7.91% to 6.64% (−16.1% relative) — Nagpur Cluster and WCC Cluster both saw Conversion Rate fall by more than 3 percentage points, the two steepest regional drops.

        -1.55pp

        Pitch‑to‑Close fell (14.68%→13.13%) — Nagpur Cluster's P to C collapsed 19.45pp (33.22%→13.77%) and WCC's fell 5.62pp (18.84%→13.22%), while North Cluster (+4.33pp) and Law + New Businesses (+2.19pp) both improved.

        -13.40%

        Productivity fell. Head Count grew 23.85% (130→161) — more than three times revenue's 7.25% growth — so Productivity (Revenue per Boe) fell from ₹1,19,408 to ₹1,03,404, a real decline in output per person.

        Law+NB +72.85%

        Fastest‑growing region, with efficiency intact. Law + New Businesses revenue grew from ₹20.66L to ₹35.71L, while both Conversion Rate (+2.19pp) and P to C (+2.19pp) improved — a rare combination of strong growth and an efficiency gain.

        WCC -54.65%

        Steepest regional decline by far. WCC Cluster revenue fell from ₹33.58L to ₹15.23L even as campaigns grew 38.8% — Conversion Rate fell 3.38pp and P to C fell 5.62pp, the clearest underperformer in the report.

        South Cluster flat

        The largest region held steady but efficiency softened. South Cluster revenue was essentially unchanged (−0.91%) even as leads and campaigns both grew strongly — Table 3 shows PackHouse improved efficiency while FightClub's Conversion Rate and P to C both fell sharply.

        MWPU -₹5.60L

        Biggest single college swing. Mit World Peace University Pune, last year's top college by revenue (₹7.54L), fell to ₹1.94L this year — a −₹5.60L drop, exactly offset by Lovely Professional University's +₹5.60L gain, the two largest moves of any matched college.
