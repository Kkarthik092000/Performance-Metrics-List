          Insights

          Key Takeaways

          The headline story reading all tables — including the market and daily trend data — together.
          +19.41%

          Strong revenue growth on a much leaner campaign base. Total revenue rose from ₹54.485L to ₹65.06L — leads +5.6%, units +16.2% — while total campaigns fell 26.88% (3,456→2,527) and pitches fell 7.80%. Growth came almost entirely from getting more out of fewer campaigns, not from more outreach.

          +0.59pp

          Conversion Rate improved (5.84%→6.43%) even as campaigns were cut sharply — South Cluster (+1.28pp), North Cluster (+0.72pp) and Nagpur Cluster (+0.63pp) led the gains, while Law + New Businesses (−0.14pp) and Medical Cluster (−0.10pp) softened only marginally.

          +2.45pp

          Pitch‑to‑Close improved sharply (9.43%→11.88%) — North Cluster gained 5.57pp (11.49%→17.06%) by far the largest P to C gain of any region, with every single region improving on this measure.

          +42.41%

          Revenue per Boe surged. Head Count fell 16.15% (161→135) while revenue grew strongly (19.41%), so Revenue per Boe rose from ₹33,842 to ₹48,193 — a large productivity gain driven by both fewer people and more revenue.

          South +59.63%

          Fastest‑growing region, with efficiency intact. South Cluster revenue grew from ₹15.58L to ₹24.87L, on Head Count held exactly flat (45→45) — Conversion Rate rose 1.28pp and P to C rose 2.74pp, driven mainly by FightClub's +81.23% sub‑cluster surge.

          WCC -16.99%

          Only region to decline. WCC Cluster revenue fell from ₹5.12L to ₹4.25L even as campaigns were cut 31.01% — but Conversion Rate still improved slightly (5.04%→5.14%) and P to C still improved (6.65%→7.72%), suggesting the decline is a scale effect rather than an efficiency problem.

          270 → 264

          The college market held roughly steady while revenue grew far faster. Active colleges fell 2.22%, in sharp contrast to the 19.41% revenue growth. 62 colleges repeated from Aug 1–13 into Sep 1–14, with their combined revenue growing 29.57% — 202 new colleges contributed 65.4% of Sep's revenue.

          Titans +241.5%

          Biggest team‑level mover. Titans (Nagpur) grew +241.51% (₹0.53L→₹1.81L), the largest percentage gain in the dataset, while Genesis (WCC) fell −63.95% (₹1.72L→₹0.62L) and T‑Samriddhi (WCC) fell to zero revenue — the two steepest declines, both concentrated in WCC Cluster.
