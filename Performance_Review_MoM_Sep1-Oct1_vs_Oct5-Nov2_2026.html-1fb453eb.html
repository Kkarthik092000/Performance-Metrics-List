Sep 1 – Oct 1, 2026 → Oct 5 – Nov 2, 2026 · Month‑over‑Month · 26 vs 25 working days

Revenue falls 21.1% with one fewer working day, as a smaller team converts fewer leads at lower rates.

Medical (+36.06%) was the only region to grow; Law + New Businesses lost ₹17.26L, South ₹11.07L, Nagpur ₹6.865L and WCC ₹5.72L, with North nearly flat.
