Sep 1 – 17, 2026 → Oct 5 – 20, 2026 · Month‑over‑Month · 13 working days each

Revenue falls 14.3% on the same 13 working days, as more leads and campaigns convert less with a smaller team.

With 13 working days in each period, revenue fell from ₹80.77L (Sep 1 – 17) to ₹69.24L (Oct 5 – 20), down 14.28% — and down 14.28% per working day (₹6,21,308→₹5,32,577). Leads (+6.14%) and campaigns (+15.07%) grew and pitches held (+1.23%), but Conversion Rate dropped from 6.25% to 5.30% and P to C from 11.19% to 9.96%, so units fell 9.94% (1,379→1,242) and AOV slipped 4.82%. Head Count shrank 10.62% (160→143), cushioning Productivity (Revenue per Boe) to a 4.09% decline. Medical Cluster (+71.31%) was the only region to grow; WCC Cluster (−74.57%) and Nagpur Cluster (−36.03%) fell the most. This report covers the overall funnel, all six regions, the sub‑clusters, a team‑level table, market‑level revenue, and day‑by‑day trends.

          Insights

          Key Takeaways

          The headline story reading all tables — including the market and daily trend data — together.
          -14.28%

          Revenue fell sharply on equal working days. Total revenue dropped from ₹80.77L to ₹69.24L (−₹11.54L); Revenue/Day fell from ₹6,21,308 to ₹5,32,577. With 13 working days in each period, this is a real decline.

          Pitches +1.23%

          More leads and campaigns did not become more pitches. Leads (+6.14%) and campaigns (+15.07%) grew, but pitches only edged up from 12,319 to 12,471 and Pitches/Day from 948 to 959 — the funnel is leaking before the pitch stage.

          Conv -0.95pp

          Conversion Rate fell (6.25%→5.30%) and P to C fell 1.23pp (11.19%→9.96%). Only Medical Cluster (+0.87pp) improved; Law + New Businesses (−1.99pp), North (−1.71pp) and WCC (−1.38pp) fell most.

          Medical +71.31%

          Medical Cluster is the bright spot. Revenue grew from ₹7.825L to ₹13.405L with no change in Head Count; Productivity rose 71.3%, pitches rose 61.3% and all five teams grew.

          WCC -74.57%

          WCC Cluster has stalled. Revenue fell from ₹5.27L to ₹1.34L and three of its five teams had zero revenue — alone, a ₹3.93L swing equal to 34% of the company's decline.

          South -18.44%

          South Cluster, the largest region, fell ₹5.68L. FightClub (−27.45%, −₹4.99L) drove it while PackHouse (−5.47%) was resilient; campaigns rose 40.7% but pitches fell 15.0%.

          Team: 10 of 32 grew

          Declines were broad at team level. 21 of 32 teams declined; Apex (+₹1.68L), Rebels 2.0 (+₹1.65L), IGNYT and T‑Orion (+₹0.98L each) were the largest gainers, T‑Pushkar (−₹2.41L), Poseidon (−₹1.75L), FC Cholas (−₹1.73L) and Aces (−₹1.46L) the largest decliners.

          Prod -4.09%

          A smaller team cushioned Productivity. Head Count fell 10.62% (160→143), so Productivity (Revenue per Boe) slipped only 4.09% (₹50,481→₹48,416) even as revenue fell 14.28%; only Medical improved it materially.

          New 73.66%

          The college base turned over heavily. 287 new colleges supplied 73.66% of Oct 5 – 20 revenue while only 80 colleges repeated — and their combined revenue fell 40.46%. The prior top‑3 colleges were absent.
