          Insights

          Key Takeaways

          The headline story reading all tables — including the market and daily trend data — together.
          +28.50%

          Broad revenue growth on a leaner campaign base. Total revenue rose from ₹83.90L to ₹1.08Cr — leads +17.9%, pitches +17.3%, units +23.1% — while total campaigns actually fell 9.7% (6,160→5,561). Growth came from doing more with fewer campaigns, not more of them.

          +0.27pp

          Conversion Rate improved from 6.18% to 6.45% even as the funnel scaled up sharply — driven mainly by Law + New Businesses (+1.57pp) and Medical Cluster (+1.44pp), offsetting a mild dip in Nagpur, South and WCC.

          +0.51pp

          Pitch‑to‑Close also improved (10.25%→10.76%) — Law + New Businesses gained 3.88pp (9.46%→13.34%) and North Cluster gained 1.71pp (11.46%→13.17%), more than offsetting declines in Nagpur (−1.11pp), South (−0.24pp) and WCC (−2.73pp).

          +25.31%

          Revenue per Boe rose sharply. Head Count grew just 2.55% (157→161) while revenue grew ten times as fast (28.50%), so Revenue per Boe rose from ₹53,436 to ₹66,960 — a genuine productivity gain across the full 21‑working‑day period.

          Law+NB +79.12%

          Fastest‑growing region, with efficiency intact. Law + New Businesses revenue nearly doubled, from ₹13.79L to ₹24.70L, even as campaigns fell 9.84% — Conversion Rate rose 1.57pp and P to C rose 3.88pp, the strongest combination of growth and efficiency in the report.

          WCC -10.96%

          Only region to decline. WCC Cluster revenue fell from ₹11.00L to ₹9.79L even as leads grew 5.8% — Conversion Rate fell from 6.67% to 5.48% and P to C fell from 10.48% to 7.75%, the steepest efficiency drop in Table 2.

          419 → 398

          The college market narrowed slightly but revenue still grew strongly. Active colleges fell 5.01%, in sharp contrast to the 28.50% revenue growth — 134 colleges repeated from Jul 2–22 into Aug 2–23 (out of 419 active in July), with 263 new colleges contributing 53.1% of Aug's revenue.

          T-Orion +122.3%

          Biggest team‑level mover, in both directions. T‑Orion (Law + New Businesses) grew +122.26% (₹2.74L→₹6.09L), the largest gain in the dataset, while T‑Samriddhi (WCC) fell −71.82% (₹1.10L→₹0.31L) and Poseidon (WCC) fell close behind at −53.27% (₹4.28L→₹2.00L).
