        Key Takeaways

        The headline story reading all tables — including the market and daily trend data — together.
        +22.56%

        Solid revenue growth on a much bigger funnel. Total revenue rose from ₹53.08L to ₹65.06L — but leads grew 32.6% and campaigns grew 27.2%, both ahead of revenue's own growth rate. The funnel scaled faster than revenue did.

        -0.10pp

        Conversion Rate held almost flat (6.53%→6.43%) — Nagpur Cluster's Conversion Rate collapsed 9.39pp, by far the steepest regional drop, while Law + New Businesses (+2.06pp) and North Cluster (+1.74pp) both improved.

        -1.62pp

        Pitch‑to‑Close fell (13.50%→11.88%) — Nagpur Cluster's P to C collapsed 22.42pp (34.95%→12.53%) and WCC's fell 10.11pp (17.83%→7.72%), while North Cluster gained 8.41pp, by far the largest improvement of any region.

        +8.94%

        Productivity still improved. Head Count grew 12.50% (120→135), about half of revenue's 22.56% growth, so Productivity (Revenue per Boe) rose from ₹44,237 to ₹48,193 despite the softer Conversion Rate and P to C.

        North +79.81%

        Fastest‑growing region, with the strongest efficiency gain too. North Cluster revenue grew from ₹4.31L to ₹7.75L, while both Conversion Rate (+1.74pp) and P to C (+8.41pp) improved sharply — the best combination of growth and efficiency anywhere in this report.

        WCC -69.11%

        Steepest regional decline by far. WCC Cluster revenue collapsed from ₹13.76L to ₹4.25L even as Head Count nearly halved (26→16) — Conversion Rate fell 3.43pp and P to C fell 10.11pp, the clearest underperformer in the report.

        PackHouse +201%

        Dramatic sub‑cluster reversal. Within South Cluster, PackHouse revenue tripled (₹3.45L→₹10.39L, ▲201.16%) while improving Conversion Rate and P to C, far outpacing FightClub's more modest +50.05% growth — PackHouse is now the dominant growth driver in South Cluster.

        Nagpur P2C -22.42pp

        Steepest efficiency collapse. Nagpur Cluster's leads exploded from 787 to 2,742 (+248.41%) while Conversion Rate fell from 14.61% to 5.22% and P to C fell from 34.95% to 12.53% — the clearest case of scale outrunning efficiency in this report.
