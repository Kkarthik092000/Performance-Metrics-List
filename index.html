        Key Takeaways

        The headline story reading all four tables together.
        -2.20%

        Revenue slipped despite more headcount. Total revenue fell from ₹34,85,000 to ₹34,08,500 while headcount rose 6.8% (133→142) — revenue per head declined materially more than the topline number alone suggests.

        -1.41pp

        Pitch‑to‑close is the weak link. Pitches rose 8.1% (5,932→6,413) but closes did not keep pace, dragging P to C from 11.26% to 9.85%. New Businesses (‑3.6pp) and Genesis‑led WCC teams drove much of the erosion.

        +3.38%

        AOV cushioned the decline. Average Order Value rose from ₹5,217 to ₹5,393 even as Total Units fell 5.4% (668→632) — July's revenue leaned on higher ticket size rather than volume.

        WCC +24.6%

        Fastest‑growing region. WCC Cluster revenue rose from ₹3.37L to ₹4.20L on leads nearly doubling (573→1,086) — the strongest, most lead‑driven growth story in the set, led by Poseidon (+55.3%).

        North -18.75%

        Steepest regional decline. North Cluster revenue fell from ₹3.68L to ₹2.99L with leads, campaigns and units all contracting together — a broad pullback, not a single weak metric.

        Aces 2.0 +288%

        Biggest team‑level mover. Aces 2.0 (Nagpur) grew revenue from ₹17,000 to ₹66,000, while three new teams (FC Fury, T‑Samriddhi, T‑Hiba) launched from zero — offsetting steep declines at Mavericks (‑47.2%) and T‑Pushkar (‑44.8%).
