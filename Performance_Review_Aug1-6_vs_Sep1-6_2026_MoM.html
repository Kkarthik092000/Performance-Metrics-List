          Insights

          Key Takeaways

          The headline story reading all tables — including the market and daily trend data — together.
          +32.44%

          Strong revenue growth on a much leaner campaign base. Total revenue rose from ₹19.68L to ₹26.07L — leads +12.9%, units +28.7% — while total campaigns fell 30.28% (1,443→1,006) and pitches held flat. Growth came almost entirely from getting more out of fewer campaigns, not from more outreach.

          +0.72pp

          Conversion Rate improved (5.15%→5.87%) even as campaigns were cut sharply — North Cluster (+3.07pp) and South Cluster (+1.54pp) led the gains, while Law + New Businesses (−2.09pp) and Nagpur (−0.50pp) were the two regions that softened.

          +2.74pp

          Pitch‑to‑Close improved sharply (9.09%→11.83%) — North Cluster gained 9.47pp (10.19%→19.66%) by far the largest P to C gain of any region, with only Law + New Businesses (−1.52pp) moving the other way.

          +57.95%

          Revenue per Boe surged. Head Count fell 16.15% (161→135) while revenue grew strongly (32.44%), so Revenue per Boe rose from ₹12,224 to ₹19,307 — a large productivity gain driven by both fewer people and more revenue.

          South +88.41%

          Fastest‑growing region, with efficiency intact. South Cluster revenue nearly doubled, from ₹5.61L to ₹10.57L, on Head Count held exactly flat (45→45) — Conversion Rate rose 1.54pp and P to C rose 3.16pp, driven mainly by FightClub's +163.45% sub‑cluster surge.

          WCC -15.05%

          Only region to decline. WCC Cluster revenue fell from ₹2.06L to ₹1.75L even as campaigns were cut 45.59% — but Conversion Rate still improved (4.97%→5.75%) and P to C still improved (7.05%→8.26%), suggesting the decline is a scale effect rather than an efficiency problem.

          137 → 142

          The college market barely widened while revenue grew far faster. Active colleges grew just 3.65%, well behind the 32.44% revenue growth. Only 24 colleges repeated from Aug 1–6 into Sep 1–6, but their combined revenue more than doubled (+103.70%) — 118 new colleges contributed 74.7% of Sep's revenue.

          Rebels 2.0 +840%

          Biggest team‑level mover. Rebels 2.0 (Medical) grew +840.00% (₹0.05L→₹0.47L), the largest percentage gain in the dataset, while T‑Samriddhi (WCC) fell to zero revenue and T‑Orion (Law + New Businesses) fell −59.66% (₹1.76L→₹0.71L) — the two steepest declines.
