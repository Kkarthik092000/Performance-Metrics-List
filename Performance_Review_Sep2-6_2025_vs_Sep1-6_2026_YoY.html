        Insights

        Key Takeaways

        The headline story reading all tables — including the market and daily trend data — together.
        +24.47%

        Solid revenue growth on a much bigger funnel. Total revenue rose from ₹20.94L to ₹26.07L — but leads grew 42.3% and campaigns grew 47.1%, both well ahead of revenue's own growth rate. The funnel scaled faster than revenue did.

        -0.47pp

        Conversion Rate eased, from 6.34% to 5.87% (−7.4% relative) — Nagpur Cluster saw the steepest drop by far (−8.02pp), while Law + New Businesses (+1.68pp) and North Cluster (+1.74pp) both improved.

        -0.67pp

        Pitch‑to‑Close eased (12.50%→11.83%) — Nagpur Cluster's P to C collapsed 15.25pp (27.63%→12.38%) and WCC's fell 6.60pp (14.86%→8.26%), while North Cluster gained 9.23pp (10.43%→19.66%), by far the largest gain of any region.

        +10.64%

        Productivity still improved. Head Count grew 12.50% (120→135), about half of revenue's 24.47% growth, so Productivity (Revenue per Boe) rose from ₹17,450 to ₹19,307 — a genuine gain despite the softer Conversion Rate and P to C.

        North +57.53%

        Best combination of growth and efficiency. North Cluster revenue grew from ₹2.19L to ₹3.45L, while both Conversion Rate (+1.74pp) and P to C (+9.23pp) improved sharply — the clearest efficiency story in this report.

        WCC -66.60%

        Steepest regional decline by far. WCC Cluster revenue collapsed from ₹5.24L to ₹1.75L even as campaigns grew 15.6% — Conversion Rate fell 1.46pp and P to C fell 6.60pp, the clearest underperformer in the report.

        PackHouse +289%

        Dramatic sub‑cluster reversal. Within South Cluster, PackHouse revenue nearly quadrupled (₹1.03L→₹4.01L, ▲289.32%) while improving Conversion Rate and P to C, outpacing FightClub's more modest +56.56% growth — a reversal from patterns seen in other periods.

        Nagpur P2C -15.25pp

        Steepest efficiency collapse. Nagpur Cluster's leads exploded from 355 to 1,311 (+269.30%) while Conversion Rate fell from 11.83% to 3.81% and P to C fell from 27.63% to 12.38% — the clearest case of scale outrunning efficiency in this report.
