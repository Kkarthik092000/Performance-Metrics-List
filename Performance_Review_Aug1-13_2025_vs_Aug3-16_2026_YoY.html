      Insights

      Key Takeaways

      The headline story reading all tables — including the market and daily trend data — together.
      +9.37%

      Revenue grew modestly on a much bigger funnel. Total revenue rose from ₹55.62L to ₹60.83L — but leads nearly doubled (+87.6%), campaigns rose 51.2% and pitches rose 70.4%. The funnel scaled far faster than revenue did.

      -3.99pp

      Conversion Rate fell sharply, from 9.78% to 5.79% (−40.8% relative) — Nagpur Cluster and WCC Cluster both saw Conversion Rate fall by more than 12 percentage points, the two steepest regional drops.

      -5.10pp

      Pitch‑to‑Close fell sharply too (14.66%→9.56%) — WCC Cluster's P to C collapsed 23.04pp (29.73%→6.69%) and Nagpur's fell 19.43pp (28.62%→9.19%), while North Cluster (+4.69pp) was the only region where P to C improved.

      -11.69%

      Productivity fell. Head Count grew 23.85% (130→161) while revenue grew only 9.37%, so Productivity (Revenue per Boe) fell from ₹42,781 to ₹37,780 — output per person is down year‑over‑year despite the much larger team and funnel.

      North +87.02%

      Best combination of growth and efficiency. North Cluster revenue nearly doubled, from ₹4.39L to ₹8.21L, while both Conversion Rate (+0.35pp) and P to C (+4.69pp) improved — the only region where growth and efficiency moved together this year.

      WCC -60.10%

      Steepest regional decline. WCC Cluster revenue fell from ₹14.14L to ₹5.64L even as leads grew 58.4% and campaigns nearly doubled — Conversion Rate and P to C both collapsed by double‑digit percentage points, the clearest underperformer in the report.

      227 → 286

      The market widened but retention weakened. Active colleges grew 25.99%, well behind the 87.56% growth in leads. Only 65 colleges repeated from Aug 1–13, 2025 into Aug 3–16, 2026, and their combined revenue fell 26.63% — nearly two‑thirds of Aug 3–16, 2026's revenue came from colleges with no activity a year earlier.

      MWPU -₹3.95L

      Biggest single college swing. Mit World Peace University Pune, last year's top college by revenue (₹4.17L), fell to just ₹22,000 this year — a −₹3.95L drop that alone accounts for the majority of the repeat‑cohort's overall decline.
