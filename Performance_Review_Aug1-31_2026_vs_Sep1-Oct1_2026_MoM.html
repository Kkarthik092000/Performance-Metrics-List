Aug 1 – 31, 2026 → Sep 1 – Oct 1, 2026 · Month‑over‑Month · 25 vs 26 working days

Revenue slips 1.1% despite an extra working day, as a 20% pitch surge fails to convert.

Revenue eased from ₹166.48L in August (25 working days) to ₹164.64L in September (26 working days), down 1.11% — and down 4.91% per working day. Leads grew 5.49% and pitches surged 20.15%, but Conversion Rate fell from 6.63% to 6.13% and Pitch‑to‑Close fell from 13.45% to 10.93%, so more pitching produced fewer units (2,909→2,841). Head Count was flat (+1.24%), so Productivity (Revenue per Boe) fell 2.32%. South Cluster was the standout (+26.40%); WCC Cluster (−48.78%) was the steepest decliner. This report covers the overall funnel, all six regions, the Law + New Businesses and South Cluster sub‑clusters, market‑level revenue, and day‑by‑day trends.

        Insights

        Key Takeaways

        The headline story reading all tables — including the market and daily trend data — together.
        -1.11%

        Revenue slipped despite an extra working day. Total revenue eased from ₹166.48L to ₹164.64L, but per working day it fell 4.91% (₹6,65,920→₹6,33,231) — September's 26th day masked a real daily slowdown.

        P to C -2.52pp

        Pitches surged but did not close. Pitches grew 20.15% (21,623→25,981) while Pitch‑to‑Close fell from 13.45% to 10.93%; every one of the six regions saw P to C fall, led by WCC (−7.23pp) and Medical (−4.51pp).

        -0.50pp

        Conversion Rate eased (6.63%→6.13%) — South Cluster (+0.15pp) was the only region to improve; Nagpur (−1.36pp) and WCC (−1.35pp) fell the most.

        -2.32%

        Productivity fell with revenue. Head Count stayed nearly flat (161→163, +1.24%), so Productivity (Revenue per Boe) fell from ₹1,03,403.73 to ₹1,01,006.13 — a revenue effect rather than a staffing one.

        South +26.40%

        Strongest region, driven by both sub‑clusters. South Cluster revenue grew from ₹48.465L to ₹61.26L with Conversion Rate up 0.15pp; FightClub (+33.84%) and PackHouse (+16.33%) both grew, and Productivity rose 11.53%.

        WCC -48.78%

        Steepest regional decline by far. WCC Cluster revenue fell from ₹15.17L to ₹7.77L as Head Count shrank from 20 to 12 — and its P to C fell 7.23pp, the sharpest of any region.

        New Biz +37.83%

        A split inside Law + New Businesses. New Businesses grew 37.83% (₹17.50L→₹24.12L) while Law fell 14.64% (₹18.27L→₹15.595L) — netting an 11.03% regional gain.

        North Prod -37.76%

        Steepest productivity decline. North Cluster revenue fell 15.80% while Head Count grew 35.29% (17→23), pulling Productivity from ₹1,27,147 to ₹79,130 — headcount outrunning output.
