          Insights

          Key Takeaways

          The headline story reading all tables — including the market and daily trend data — together.
          +2.95%

          Modest headline growth on a much leaner, more selective funnel. Total revenue rose from ₹90.97L to ₹93.66L — leads +2.18%, but units were essentially flat (−0.38%) — while total campaigns fell 22.86% (5,001→3,858) and pitches fell 5.10%. Growth this period came from AOV (+3.34%), not from higher volume.

          -0.16pp

          Conversion Rate eased slightly (6.33%→6.17%) — South Cluster (+0.83pp) was the only region to improve, while Nagpur Cluster fell sharpest (−1.38pp), followed by Medical Cluster (−0.67pp) and WCC Cluster (−0.51pp).

          +0.55pp

          Pitch‑to‑Close improved overall (11.15%→11.70%) — North Cluster gained 2.15pp (12.01%→14.16%), the largest P to C gain of any region, with South (+1.71pp) and Law + New Businesses (+0.78pp) also improving even as Medical, Nagpur and WCC softened.

          +22.78%

          Revenue per Boe surged. Head Count fell 16.15% (161→135) while revenue still grew (2.95%), so Revenue per Boe rose from ₹56,503 to ₹69,374 — the standout productivity gain in this report, driven almost entirely by the leaner team.

          South +42.12%

          Fastest‑growing region, with efficiency intact. South Cluster revenue grew from ₹25.07L to ₹35.63L, on Head Count held exactly flat (45→45) — Conversion Rate rose 0.83pp and P to C rose 1.71pp, driven mainly by FightClub's +51.5% sub‑cluster surge.

          4 of 6 regions declined

          South's surge masked broad regional softness. WCC Cluster fell steepest (−34.36%, ₹8.15L→₹5.35L), followed by Medical (−18.39%), Nagpur (−17.10%) and North (−6.81%) — only South and a near‑flat Law + New Businesses (+1.54%) avoided a decline.

          367 → 334

          The college market shrank while revenue still edged up. Active colleges fell 8.99%, a sharper decline than the 2.95% revenue growth. Only 97 colleges repeated from Aug 1–20 into Sep 1–20, with their combined revenue essentially flat (−1.27%) — 237 new colleges contributed 58.1% of Sep's revenue.

          PH Shadow Fangs +143.1%

          Biggest team‑level mover. PH Shadow Fangs (South) grew +143.10% (₹1.16L→₹2.82L), the largest percentage gain in the dataset, while Genesis (WCC) fell −73.93% (₹2.57L→₹0.67L) and T‑Samriddhi (WCC) fell to zero revenue — the two steepest declines, both concentrated in WCC Cluster.
