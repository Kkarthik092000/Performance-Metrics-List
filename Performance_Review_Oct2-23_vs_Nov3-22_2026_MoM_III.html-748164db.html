Revenue falls 24.7% on a smaller pitch funnel, even as pitch‑to‑close rises to 10.12%.

Across 16 vs 16 reported working days, revenue fell ₹82.66L→₹62.26L (-24.68%, -₹20.40L). Campaigns fell 13.96%, leads 25.60% and pitches 33.85%; units fell 24.33% while AOV moved -0.47%.
