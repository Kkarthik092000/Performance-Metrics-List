Revenue up 10.8%, but conversion and pitch‑to‑close both fell hard.

Revenue grew 10.79% year‑over‑year on a much bigger funnel — campaigns up 29.4%, leads up 42.7%, pitches up 52.7%, units up 13.2%. That scale came at a real efficiency cost: Conversion Rate fell from 8.56% to 6.78% and Pitch‑to‑Close dropped from 14.41% to 10.68%. Headcount grew 29.8%, faster than revenue, so Revenue per Boe fell 14.6%. This report covers the overall funnel, all six regions, market‑level revenue (overall and by region), and day‑by‑day trends.

      Insights

      Key Takeaways

      The headline story reading all tables — including the market and daily trend data — together.
      +10.79%

      Revenue grew on volume, not efficiency. Total revenue rose from ₹1.27Cr to ₹1.41Cr — but it took 42.7% more leads, 29.4% more campaigns and 52.7% more pitches to get there. This was a scale‑driven year, not an efficiency‑driven one.

      -3.73pp

      Pitch‑to‑close is the biggest casualty. P to C fell from 14.41% to 10.68% (‑25.9% relative) — WCC Cluster alone dropped nearly 27 points (37.10%→10.22%), the steepest efficiency collapse in the table.

      -1.78pp

      Conversion Rate also fell hard. Conversion dropped from 8.56% to 6.78% (‑20.8% relative) — Nagpur Cluster alone fell 8.46pp (15.31%→6.85%), the sharpest regional decline.

      -14.62%

      Productivity per head declined sharply. Head Count grew 29.75% (121→157) while revenue grew only 10.79%, so Productivity (Revenue/Head) fell from ₹1,04,944 to ₹89,605 — the team expanded far faster than the topline it produced.

      South +23.73%

      Fastest‑growing region in absolute terms. South Cluster revenue rose from ₹36.16L to ₹44.74L — already the largest region — on leads up 17.2% and pitches up 24.5%, while Conversion Rate eased only slightly (8.43%→8.11%).

      WCC -36.58%

      Steepest regional decline. WCC Cluster revenue fell from ₹26.99L to ₹17.12L despite leads growing 52.2% — a region that pulled in far more leads but converted and closed them dramatically less effectively than a year ago (P2C 37.10%→10.22%).

      464 → 573

      The college market widened sharply. Active colleges grew 23.49% — 421 new colleges contributed ₹72.83L (51.8% of July 2026's revenue), while 312 colleges from July 2025 (₹62.06L, 48.9% of that year's revenue) didn't repeat. This is a highly transient market: repeat colleges themselves grew only 3.53%.
