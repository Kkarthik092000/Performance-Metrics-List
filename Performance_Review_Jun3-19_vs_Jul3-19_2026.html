        Key Takeaways

        The headline story reading all four tables together.
        +9.98%

        Strongest revenue growth in recent reports. Total revenue rose from ₹61,13,000 to ₹67,23,000 on more campaigns (+8.6%), more pitches (+12.8%) and a roughly flat lead base (‑1.7%) — growth came from working the funnel harder, not from more raw leads.

        +0.48pp

        Conversion Rate improved. Conversion rose from 5.70% to 6.18%, an 8.4% relative gain — North Cluster (+1.62pp) and Medical Cluster (+2.06pp) led the improvement.

        -0.59pp

        Pitch‑to‑close eased slightly. P to C dipped from 10.84% to 10.25% even as Conversion Rate improved — the back end of the funnel (pitch → close) is under more strain than the front end (lead → conversion).

        -5.63%

        Headcount outgrew revenue. Head Count rose 16.5% (133→155) against 9.98% revenue growth, so Revenue Per Boe fell from ₹45,962 to ₹43,374 — output per person is down even though the topline is up.

        WCC +40.1%

        Fastest‑growing region. WCC Cluster revenue rose from ₹6.34L to ₹8.89L on leads up 54.7% and campaigns up 57.8% — the most aggressive, and most successful, scale‑up in the set.

        Law+NB -20.4%

        Steepest regional decline. Law + New Businesses revenue fell from ₹13.63L to ₹10.85L, driven mainly by Law (‑30.3%, within Table 3) and Athena/T‑Pushkar at the team level — a broad pullback across this region's funnel.
