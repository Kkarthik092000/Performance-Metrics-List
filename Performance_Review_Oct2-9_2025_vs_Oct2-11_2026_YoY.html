Oct 2 – 9, 2025 → Oct 2 – 11, 2026 · Year‑over‑Year · 6 working days each

Revenue up 11.0% year over year on equal working days, but conversion and ticket size slipped as volume surged.

With 6 working days in each period, revenue grew from ₹29.88L (Oct 2 – 9, 2025) to ₹33.16L (Oct 2 – 11, 2026), up 10.98%. Leads rose 60.52% and pitches 47.64%, but Conversion Rate fell from 6.91% to 5.39% and Average Order Value from ₹6,356 to ₹5,639 (−11.29%), so units grew 25.11% (470→588) while revenue grew less than half as fast as leads. Head Count grew 16.26% (123→143), so Productivity (Revenue per Boe) fell 4.55%. Medical Cluster (+367.78%) and North Cluster (+85.22%) drove growth; WCC Cluster (−93.49%) was the only region to decline. This report covers the overall funnel, all six regions, the South Cluster sub‑clusters, market‑level revenue, and day‑by‑day trends.

        Key Takeaways

        The headline story reading all tables — including the market and daily trend data — together.
        +10.98%

        Real growth on equal working days. Revenue grew from ₹29.88L to ₹33.16L (+₹3.28L) and Revenue/Day from ₹4,97,917 to ₹5,52,583, with 6 working days in each period.

        AOV -11.29%

        Growth is volume, not value. Units grew 25.11% but AOV fell from ₹6,356 to ₹5,639, so revenue grew less than half as fast as units — more, smaller sales.

        Conv -1.52pp

        Conversion Rate fell (6.91%→5.39%) as leads grew 60.52% — South (−2.06pp), Nagpur (−2.75pp) and Law + New Businesses (−22.68pp, distorted by a very small 2025 lead base) fell; Medical improved by 4.42pp.

        P to C -2.17pp

        Pitch‑to‑Close weakened (14.19%→12.02%) despite 47.64% more pitches; Medical rose 15.36pp to 20.25% and North 1.64pp, while Nagpur (−11.35pp) and South (−2.44pp) fell.

        Medical +367.78%

        Medical Cluster is the growth engine. Revenue rose from ₹1.195L to ₹5.59L with P to C at 20.25% and Productivity up 266% — it is now 16.9% of revenue, from 4.0%.

        North +85.22%

        North Cluster nearly doubled. Revenue grew from ₹2.30L to ₹4.26L with units doubling, P to C up 1.64pp and Productivity up 26.29% — the only region where growth outpaced added headcount (alongside Medical).

        WCC -93.49%

        WCC Cluster is the only decline. Revenue fell from ₹5.84L to ₹0.38L (−₹5.46L) as Head Count fell from 22 to 6 — a drag bigger than the company's entire net gain, though 43% of the 2025 base was one ₹2.5L unlisted entry.

        South +12.59%

        South's growth was masked by South Core's exit. FightClub (+73.51%) and PackHouse (+427.18%) added ₹7.48L, but South Core's ₹6.06L 2025 revenue disappeared, leaving net growth of ₹1.42L and Productivity down 22.73%.

        Prod -4.55%

        Headcount grew faster than revenue. Head Count rose 16.26% (123→143) against 10.98% revenue growth, so Productivity (Revenue per Boe) slipped from ₹24,289 to ₹23,185.

        New 81.84%

        Four‑fifths of revenue came from new colleges. 177 colleges with no prior‑year revenue supplied 81.84% of revenue while only 22 of 134 colleges repeated; repeat‑college revenue rose 8.66%.
